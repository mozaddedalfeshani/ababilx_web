## 1. Purpose and Scope

AbabilX brings people together in chats, groups, and workspaces (Board and Wall). These Guidelines set the behavioral standard inside them. They apply to everyone in a group or workspace and complement the [Acceptable Use Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md) ("AUP"), which lists platform-wide prohibitions. Where these Guidelines and the AUP overlap, the stricter rule applies.

## 2. Definitions

Terms defined in the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) apply. "Team Space" means a workspace's Board and Wall. "Group" means a group chat.

## 3. Core Expectations

1. **Be professional.** Team Spaces are working environments. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. **Be honest.** Do not fabricate work records, misattribute contributions, or misrepresent activity (AUP items 53, 72).
3. **Respect boundaries.** Colleagues' personal information, schedules, and off-hours are not yours to broadcast.
4. **Respect access.** Use only the Team data your role grants; leaving a Team ends your right to its content (AUP item 94).
5. **Assume good faith first, report real problems fast.** Most friction is misunderstanding; genuine misconduct should be reported, not endured (Section 9).

## 4. Team Spaces

- **Wall:** for posts relevant to the team — announcements, discussion, appreciation. Keep categories on-topic; category requests are reviewed by team moderators.
- **Board (kanban):** task titles, descriptions, and comments should describe work accurately. Do not delete or move others' tasks to obscure history or shift blame.
- **Workspace and chat are separate:** a workspace gives no one access to your chats.

## 5. Content Standards

Do not post in any Team Space content that:

1. harasses, demeans, threatens, or discriminates (AUP Section 4.7);
2. is sexually explicit or gratuitously violent;
3. discloses another person's private information without consent — including screenshots of private conversations shared to shame;
4. is deliberately false and presented as fact about a person or their work;
5. is spam, chain content, or off-platform promotion unrelated to the team's work;
6. infringes intellectual-property rights (see [Copyright & DMCA Policy](./copyright-dmca-policy.md));
7. contains credentials, tokens, or secrets — including your own. Post secrets nowhere; rotate any secret posted by accident and tell your team owner.

## 6. Chats and Groups

1. **End-to-end encryption means we cannot see what is said** — so the people in a chat are the first line of defence. Block anyone, and report a conversation from inside the app.
2. **Group admins set the rules of their group** — who may post, add people, or change its details — and must not use those powers to bully members.
3. **Do not add people to groups they did not ask to join** to promote, spam, or harass them.
4. **Respect a message request.** If someone does not accept or replies by blocking, do not reach them through new accounts.

## 7. Roles and Moderation Within Teams

1. Team owners and managers moderate their own Team Spaces first: they can manage categories, remove content, adjust roles, and remove members.
2. Moderation power carries obligations — apply rules consistently, don't use role permissions to bully, silence good-faith dissent, or snoop beyond your legitimate function.
3. AbabilX intervenes at the platform level when conduct violates the AUP or these Guidelines and the Team cannot or will not address it, or when the violator controls the Team.

## 8. Privacy Features

Privacy Mode, private chat, view-once and timed photos, Ghost Chat, and Local Share protect people. Using them to harass, pressure, threaten, or exploit someone violates these Guidelines and the AUP (Section 4.4), and is grounds for suspension.

## 9. Reporting

- **Inside your Team:** raise it with your team owner or a manager, who has the tools to act.
- **To AbabilX:** email `info@ababilx.com` with the team, content, and what happened — especially when the problem involves the team's leadership, or is severe (threats, hate speech, doxxing, CSAM — the last is reported to authorities without exception).
- Reports are handled confidentially; retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.

## 10. Enforcement

Violations are handled under the AUP's enforcement framework ([AUP](./acceptable-use-policy.md), Section 7): content removal, feature restrictions, suspension, or termination, proportionate to severity and history. Team-level moderation by owners is independent of, and does not limit, platform-level enforcement. Appeals follow AUP Section 8.
