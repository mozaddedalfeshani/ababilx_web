## 1. Introduction

AbabilX ("AbabilX," "we," "us," or "our") is a private messenger: one-to-one and group chats, voice and video calls, file sharing, Ghost Chat, and Local Share, on Android, iPhone, web, and desktop. Teams can also use the AbabilX Workspace (Section 4). Together these are the "Service."

This Policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It is written to be read. The short version:

- **We cannot read your messages.** They are locked on your device with keys we never hold.
- **We do not keep your messages.** Our servers deliver a message and delete it once your devices have it.
- **We need almost nothing to run your account.** Email and phone number are optional.
- **No AI.** AbabilX runs no AI models on your messages, files, calls, or workspace content, and never uses them to train one.
- **No ads, no trackers, no selling your data. Ever.** We do not embed advertising, analytics, or profiling SDKs in any website or app, and we do not track you across other sites.

## 2. How We Protect Your Chats

### 2.1 End-to-End Encryption

Direct messages, group messages, and your Note to Self are end-to-end encrypted. Each message is locked on the sender's device with a key that exists only on the devices of the people in that conversation. Our servers only ever carry the locked form. **Because we do not hold the keys, there is nothing we could give a government, a military, a court, or an attacker who breaks into our systems.**

- **Photos, videos, voice notes, and files** are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded — on the phone, the web, and desktop alike — each with its own key that only the people in the chat can open. Our storage holds locked data with a hidden name and type.
- **Your keys never leave your devices in a form we can open.** They are created on your device. Moving them to a new device happens directly from your phone by QR code.
- **Notifications stay locked.** On Android, a message notification arrives locked and is opened by your phone, not by us.
- **You can check for eavesdroppers.** Every conversation has a safety number. If yours matches the other person's, nobody is sitting between you. The app warns you if a contact's key ever changes.

### 2.2 Delivered, Then Deleted

AbabilX does not keep your chat history on its servers. A message waits on our servers only until every device of every person in the conversation has received it — then it is permanently deleted, along with its files. If a device never comes back, the message is deleted after **30 days** regardless. Your history lives on your own devices.

### 2.3 Ghost Chat

Ghost Chat goes directly from phone to phone. Its text and files never touch our servers and are never stored anywhere. One tap on the flame wipes it from both phones. For each photo or file you choose whether it disappears after a set time and whether the other person may save it.

### 2.4 Calls

One-to-one voice and video calls connect directly between the two devices and are encrypted by the call itself. When a network blocks a direct path, the call passes through our relay, which carries it but cannot decrypt it. Group calls are encrypted in transit. **No call is recorded, and call content is never stored.**

### 2.5 Local Share

Local Share sends text, files, and folders between your devices on the same Wi-Fi network. It never passes through our servers, and we never see what you share. Each device must be accepted by you once before it can send anything.

### 2.6 What We Cannot See, and What We Need

| We **cannot** see | We need, only to deliver the Service |
|---|---|
| The text of your messages | The account details you choose to give (Section 7.1) |
| Your photos, videos, voice notes, and files | Who is in a conversation and when a message is sent, so it can be delivered |
| Your message keys | A device token, so your phone can be woken for a new message |
| Anything in Ghost Chat or Local Share | Your IP address at the moment of a connection, as every internet service does |
| What is said in your calls | |

**Exceptions.** Messages posted into a conversation by an outside integration (a webhook feed) arrive from outside AbabilX and are not end-to-end encrypted. A group carried over from an older workspace channel becomes encrypted when a member's app sets up its key. Files sent before encrypted files were introduced stay in their original form until they are deleted.

## 3. Privacy Controls You Hold

### 3.1 Start Without Email or Phone

You can create an account with **Start New Chat**: no email address, no phone number, no name required. Your account is tied to a key on your phone. You may instead sign in with Google or with an email and password. Adding an email or phone number is always optional.

**Phone numbers** are never used to sign in or to recover an account. Searching for people by phone number is currently paused.

### 3.2 Privacy Mode

One switch in Settings, free for everyone. When it is on, for your direct messages and one-to-one calls:

- **Nobody sees your activity.** People stop seeing when you are online, typing, or have read their messages. Their call screen never shows "Ringing."
- **Nobody can find you.** You disappear from username, email, and phone search, and your QR code cannot be scanned to reach you.
- **Nobody can add you to a group.**
- **Connected calls leave no trace** in the other person's chat history.
- **Screenshots are blocked** on the other person's Android phone while your chat or call is open.
- **You control every photo you send** — view once, or disappear after 5, 10, or 30 seconds, and whether it may be saved. When the timer ends, the photo is deleted from our storage and from both phones.

You can also choose, person by person, to be **always private** or **never private** with someone, whatever the switch says.

### 3.3 Private Chat

Turn on private chat in any direct conversation and it binds both sides: no forwarding, no saving media, no call history, no threads, and screenshots blocked on Android. Notifications show only "New private message."

### 3.4 Group Privacy

A group's admins can switch on group privacy: nobody can forward or save anything, members see only the admins rather than the full member list, and screenshots are blocked for every member on Android.

### 3.5 Discovery

You decide whether people can find you by username or email. New accounts are not findable by username until you turn it on.

### 3.6 Your Own Backup

On Android, AbabilX keeps an automatic backup in a folder on your phone. The backup file is never uploaded to us. It is locked with a key tied to your account, so only you, signed in, can restore it.

### 3.7 Linked Devices

You can link up to five computers or browsers to your phone. Each keeps its own copy of your chats. A linked device that is unused for 14 days is signed out automatically, and you can sign any device out at any time.

> **Good to know.** Screenshot blocking works on Android. Nothing can stop someone photographing a screen with another camera.

## 4. Workspace

AbabilX Workspace gives a team a shared **Board** and **Wall**. Workspace content is visible only to members of that workspace, according to the roles its owner sets, and the owner manages who is in it. Workspace data is encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by the same access controls and session security as the rest of the Service (Section 15), and never used for advertising or to train AI. Chats are entirely separate: a workspace grants no access to anyone's messages.

Workspaces may be on the Free or Plus plan. Plus is paid through bKash; we keep the payment record and never receive your bKash PIN.

The Service is for people aged **18 or older**. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from anyone younger. If we learn that we have, we delete it and close the Account. Contact `info@ababilx.com` if you believe a minor is using AbabilX.

## 7. Information We Collect

### 7.1 Information You Give Us

- **Account details:** a display name and username if you choose them, a profile picture if you add one, and an email address only if you sign in with email or Google. A phone number only if you add one.
- **Password:** if you set one, we store only a one-way hash (argon2id). We cannot see your password.
- **Settings:** language, theme, discovery, Privacy Mode, and notification choices.
- **Messages and files:** carried in locked form only, and deleted once delivered (Section 2.2). **Scheduled messages** (Send later) are held locked until their send time.
- **Workspace content:** board cards and wall posts you create in a workspace.
- **Payments:** workspace Plus payment records (amount, transaction ID, seats, period).
- **Support:** messages you send us.

### 7.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Signed-in devices:** device type, app version, and the IP address and time of sign-in, so you can see and sign out your own devices.
- **Delivery data:** which conversation a message belongs to, its time and size, and which of your devices have received it.
- **Push tokens:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging token per device, removed when you sign out or turn off notifications.
- **Security logs:** sign-in attempts, rate-limit events, and errors, kept briefly to stop abuse.

We use no third-party analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or device fingerprinting. Our website and web app use only the browser storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your preferences ([Cookie Policy](./cookie-policy.md)).

### 7.3 Information from Others

- **Google:** confirmation of your identity, only if you sign in with Google.
- **bKash:** confirmation of a workspace payment.

We never buy data about you.

## 8. How We Use Information

4. **Tell you what matters** — security alerts, policy changes, and reminders before an unused account is deleted (Section 12).
5. **Meet legal obligations** (Section 10.3).

We never use your information for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

## 10. How We Share Information

**Our policy is simple: we never share your data with anyone.** Not with advertisers, not with data brokers, not with partners, not with governments on request. We do not sell or rent Personal Data, and we never have. The only parties that touch your data are the ones below, and none of them can read your messages.

### 10.1 Providers Who Help Run the Service

Hosting, file storage, push notifications, and email are run by providers that only carry data on our behalf — this is not sharing. They are bound by contract to use it for nothing else ([Subprocessor List](./subprocessors.md)). File storage holds only the locked form of chat files.

### 10.2 People You Talk To

The people in a conversation see what you send them, your name, picture, and username. In a workspace, members see the content of that workspace.

### 10.3 Legal Requests

We never hand over data voluntarily or informally. Only a binding legal order can compel us, and even then we can give only the little we hold (Section 7.1). **We cannot provide the content of your messages, files, calls, Ghost Chats, or Local Share transfers to anyone, because we cannot read them and we do not keep them.** Where we are allowed to, we tell you before disclosing anything, and we challenge requests we believe are overbroad.

### 10.4 Business Transfers

If AbabilX is merged or sold, any successor must keep commitments at least as protective as this Policy, and we will tell you first.

## 13. Your Rights and Choices

You may **access, correct, delete, or export** your data, **object** to processing, **withdraw consent**, and **complain** to your data-protection authority. Most of this is in the app: edit your profile, remove your phone number, change discovery, turn on Privacy Mode, sign out devices, delete chats, and delete your Account. For anything else, email `info@ababilx.com` — we reply within 30 days and never treat you differently for asking.

## 14. Account Deletion

Delete your Account at any time in the app: **Settings → Account → Delete account** on your phone, or, for an account with no phone signed in, **Settings → Security → Delete account** on desktop. You can also email `info@ababilx.com` — see our [Account Deletion guide](/account-deletion). An Account unused for 30 days is deleted automatically. Deletion removes your profile, settings, devices, and any messages and files still waiting for delivery. It cannot be undone. Messages already on other people's phones stay on their phones. If you own a workspace, deleting your Account deletes that workspace too.

## 15. Security

- **End-to-end encryption** for messages and every chat file (Section 2.1).
- **Delivery-only storage:** a breach finds no chat history, because there is none to find.
- **Encryption in transit and at rest** for everything else (TLS, encrypted storage).
- **Short-lived sign-in tokens**, rotating refresh tokens, and instant sign-out of any device.
- **Passwords hashed** with argon2id; sign-in attempts rate-limited and locked after repeated failures.
- **Least-privilege access** for the few people who operate the Service.

Full detail: [Security Policy](./security-policy.md). Found a vulnerability? See our [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](./vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md).
