## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines conduct that is prohibited on the AbabilX Service. It applies to every user, Account, Team, and client (web, desktop, mobile, API, and any future CLI or browser extension), and to all content transmitted through or stored on the Service. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

## 2. Definitions

Terms defined in the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) and [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) have the same meanings here. "Content" includes Customer Content, Output you distribute, and anything you cause the Service to post on a third-party platform.

## 3. General Standard

Use the Service lawfully, honestly, and without harming the Service, other users, third-party platforms, or third parties. Where a specific behavior is not listed below but is of the same character as a listed prohibition, it is equally prohibited. If you are unsure whether a use is acceptable, ask `info@ababilx.com` before proceeding.

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

### 4.1 Illegal Activity

You may not use the Service to:

1. violate any applicable law or regulation;
2. store, transmit, or distribute content that is illegal in your jurisdiction or ours;
3. facilitate, promote, or provide instructions for criminal activity;
4. launder money or move proceeds of crime;
5. evade sanctions or export-control restrictions (see [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md), Section 18);
6. infringe another party's copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, or other rights;
7. violate another person's privacy or data-protection rights, including collecting Personal Data without a lawful basis;
8. distribute child sexual abuse material or any content sexualizing minors (reported to authorities without exception);
9. traffic in stolen data, credentials, or financial instruments;
10. sell or promote illegal goods or services.

### 4.2 Security Violations

You may not:

11. access or attempt to access another user's Account, data, tokens, or sessions;
12. probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service outside the [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](./vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md);
13. breach or circumvent authentication, authorization, or policy-gate controls;
14. forge, replay, tamper with, or fabricate tokens, exchange codes, connect tickets, cookies, or webhook signatures;
15. brute-force pairing codes, exchange codes, webhook tokens, or any credential;
16. intercept or monitor traffic between other users and the Service;
17. introduce malware, ransomware, spyware, keyloggers, cryptominers, logic bombs, or any malicious code;
18. use the Service to develop, host, distribute, or command-and-control malware;
19. conduct denial-of-service or resource-exhaustion attacks against the Service or any third party;
20. exploit a vulnerability for any purpose other than good-faith reporting under our disclosure policy;
21. steal, harvest, or attempt to extract credentials, API keys, or OAuth tokens of any party;
22. exfiltrate data through AI features, crafted prompts, or injection techniques;
23. attempt to extract system prompts, model configurations, or other users' AI context;
24. disable, overload, or impair any part of the Service's infrastructure.

### 4.3 Abuse of Platform Mechanics

You may not:

25. circumvent, or attempt to circumvent, rate limits, quotas, or plan limits;
26. create multiple Accounts to evade limits, bans, billing, or enforcement;
27. share one Account among multiple people to evade per-user limits;
28. automate account creation;
29. resell, sublicense, or provide third parties access to the Service without authorization;
30. use the free plan programmatically at scale to substitute for a paid plan;
31. manipulate AI usage accounting or abuse retry mechanisms to exceed allowances;
32. scrape the Service, or bulk-download content you do not own, outside documented API access;
33. use the API in violation of the [Developer & API Policy](./developer-api-policy.md);
34. cache or store other users' data beyond what a permitted integration requires;
35. interfere with the Service's operation, updates, or security mechanisms;
36. remove, obscure, or falsify attribution or provenance information the Service attaches to content;
37. abuse webhook endpoints with junk, oversized, or malicious payloads;
38. register misleading webhook integrations to capture data not intended for you.

### 4.4 Misuse of Third-Party Integrations

You may not use AbabilX's automation to violate a connected platform's terms or harm its users, including:

39. using auto-commit or scheduled automation to fabricate development activity in a deceptive manner (for example, to defraud an employer, client, or metric-based program);
40. committing to repositories you lack rights to modify;
41. posting to Slack channels or workspaces without authorization;
42. sending messages through the Service that violate Slack's or GitHub's policies;
43. using the Service to evade a suspension or ban imposed by GitHub, Google, or Slack;
44. exceeding or laundering third-party API quotas through our infrastructure;
45. connecting accounts you do not own or lack authority to connect;
46. using integration tokens obtained through AbabilX outside the Service.

### 4.5 Spam and Deceptive Practices

You may not:

47. send spam, bulk unsolicited messages, or chain content through any Service feature (chat, wall, standups, digests, webhooks);
48. use scheduled messages to flood channels or recipients;
49. engage in phishing, or create content that impersonates login pages, security notices, or other trusted communications;
50. impersonate any person, organization, or AbabilX itself;
51. misrepresent your affiliation or the origin of content;
52. distribute deceptive AI-generated content presented as human-authored where that presentation is materially misleading;
53. manipulate reactions, activity graphs, attendance records, or analytics to deceive a team or third party;
54. operate pyramid schemes, advance-fee fraud, fake giveaways, or other fraudulent schemes;
55. engage in payment fraud, including using stolen payment instruments or initiating illegitimate chargebacks;
56. abuse trials, promotions, or referral mechanics through fabricated identities.

### 4.6 Cryptocurrency and Resource Abuse

You may not:

57. mine cryptocurrency using Service infrastructure;
58. use automation features to farm airdrops, testnet rewards, or activity-based token distributions deceptively;
59. operate wallet-draining, rug-pull, or other crypto-fraud schemes through the Service;
60. use the Service as infrastructure for unregistered securities offerings or scam token promotion.

### 4.7 Harassment and Harmful Content

You may not post, store, or transmit content that:

61. harasses, bullies, threatens, or intimidates any person;
62. incites or glorifies violence against people or groups;
63. is hate speech — attacking people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, or serious disease;
64. sexualizes anyone without consent, or constitutes non-consensual intimate imagery;
65. doxxes — publishing another's private information (home address, government ID, private contact details) without consent;
66. stalks or enables stalking or surveillance of another person;
67. encourages self-harm or suicide;
68. constitutes terrorist or violent-extremist content or recruitment;
69. defames another person with reckless or knowing falsehood;
70. exploits or endangers minors in any way.

Team-space behavioral norms are further described in our [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md).

### 4.8 Misuse of AI Features

You may not use AI Features to:

71. generate content prohibited elsewhere in this AUP;
72. produce deliberately false summaries or reports intended to deceive a team, employer, or client;
73. attempt prompt injection against the Service or other users;
74. probe, jailbreak, or manipulate models to bypass safety controls;
75. generate malware, exploits, or attack tooling for unauthorized use;
76. mass-produce misleading or spam content;
77. represent Output as an audit, certification, or professional opinion;
78. process content you have no right to process (see [AI Policy](./ai-policy.md), Section 14).

### 4.9 Reverse Engineering and Competitive Misuse

You may not:

79. reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service except where law grants an unwaivable right;
80. bypass technical protections on the desktop, mobile, or web clients;
81. systematically extract Service features, prompts, or data to build or train a competing product;
82. benchmark and publish results in a misleading manner that misrepresents the Service;
83. access the Service to monitor its availability or functions on behalf of a competitor without our consent.

### 4.10 Infrastructure and Network Abuse

You may not:

84. use the Service as an open proxy, relay, or anonymization layer for attacks;
85. spoof network identifiers or headers to disguise abusive traffic;
86. distribute or coordinate botnets;
87. conduct port scanning or network reconnaissance of our systems or third parties from the Service;
88. abuse presigned upload URLs to host unrelated files, pirated content, or malware;
89. use storage features as a general-purpose CDN or file-sharing service for content unrelated to the Service's purpose;
90. embed the Service in a frame or wrapper that misleads users about what they are interacting with.

### 4.11 Data Misuse

You may not:

91. collect or harvest other users' Personal Data from the Service;
92. use team features to surveil members beyond the feature's disclosed purpose;
93. re-identify or attempt to re-identify de-identified data;
94. retain data from a Team after your authorized access ends, beyond copies you lawfully own;
95. sell or broker data obtained through the Service;
96. use attendance, activity, or analytics data to violate employment or privacy law.

### 4.12 Integrity of Legal and Billing Processes

You may not:

97. submit false DMCA notices or counter-notices (see [Copyright & DMCA Policy](./copyright-dmca-policy.md));
98. submit fraudulent privacy or data-subject requests targeting another person's data;
99. abuse support channels through threats, deception, or vexatious volume;
100. misrepresent your identity or authority in any legal, billing, or verification process;
101. evade payment through technical manipulation of plan enforcement;
102. file chargebacks for services legitimately received, in lieu of the refund process in the [Billing & Refund Policy](./billing-refund-policy.md).

## 5. Examples

> **Example — automation misuse (prohibited, items 39, 53):** configuring auto-commit to generate daily filler commits on an employer-monitored repository so contribution graphs misrepresent work performed.
>
> **Example — permitted automation:** using auto-commit on your own experimental repository to keep a scheduled changelog file updated, with no one being deceived.

> **Example — rate-limit evasion (prohibited, items 25–26):** after receiving HTTP 429 responses, rotating through several accounts or IP addresses to continue hammering the sign-in endpoint.
>
> **Example — permitted retry:** honoring `Retry-After` and backing off exponentially.

> **Example — AI misuse (prohibited, item 72):** editing a standup rule so the AI reports completed work on tickets no one touched, to mislead a client.
>
> **Example — permitted use:** letting the AI draft your standup from real commit activity, then reviewing and correcting it before it posts.

> **Example — security research:** testing whether our WebSocket handshake leaks tokens **is** in scope for good-faith research under the [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](./vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md); testing it by hijacking another user's live session **is not** (item 11).

## 6. Reporting Violations

Report suspected violations to `info@ababilx.com` (or `info@ababilx.com` for security matters) with the relevant account, content, or URL and a description. We review reports promptly and keep reporter identities confidential except where disclosure is legally required.

## 7. Enforcement

We may, at our discretion and proportionate to the violation:

1. remove or disable content;
2. pause or delete offending automations (rules, jobs, webhooks);
3. throttle, suspend, or restrict features of an Account or Team;
4. suspend or terminate Accounts (see [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md), Section 19);
5. revoke API or integration access;
6. notify affected third-party platforms;
7. report to law enforcement where we believe conduct is criminal, and preserve related records.

For minor, curable violations we will ordinarily warn first. Violations involving illegality, security attacks, minors, or imminent harm result in immediate action without notice.

## 8. Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was mistaken, reply to the enforcement notice or write to `info@ababilx.com` within 30 days with the facts you believe we got wrong. A person not involved in the original decision will review the appeal and respond.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this AUP as new abuse patterns emerge, recording changes in Section 11. The version in force at the time of the conduct governs enforcement.
