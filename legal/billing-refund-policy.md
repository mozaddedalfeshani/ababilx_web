## 1. Scope

This policy covers payments for AbabilX and forms part of our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md). Terms defined there apply here.

## 2. What Is Free

**AbabilX Chat is free.** Messages, groups, voice and video calls, Ghost Chat, Local Share, Privacy Mode, and every other chat feature cost nothing, with no paid tier and no limits sold back to you. We do not show ads and do not sell data to pay for it.

## 3. Workspace Plus

A Workspace runs on **Free** or **Plus**. Plus raises the Workspace's limits (boards and storage), as shown in the Workspace's billing page at the time of purchase. Plus is priced **per member seat, per month**, in Bangladeshi taka (BDT). The price shown before you pay is the price you pay.

## 4. How Payment Works

1. The Workspace owner opens billing, chooses **1 to 12 months**, and sees the total: seats × months × the monthly seat price.
2. The owner pays that amount through **bKash** and submits the bKash transaction ID and sender number.
3. We check the payment and approve it, usually within one business day. Plus starts on approval. If Plus is already active, the new months are added to the end.
4. If something does not match, the request is put on hold or declined with a reason, and the owner can correct and resubmit it.

We receive only the transaction ID, sender number, and amount. **Your bKash PIN never reaches us.** Each transaction ID can be used once.

## 5. No Automatic Renewal

Plus **never renews by itself** and nothing is ever charged without you paying it. When the paid months end, you choose whether to pay again.

## 6. When Plus Ends

The Workspace returns to Free. **Nothing is deleted.** While the Workspace is above the Free limits, you cannot add more boards or files until you renew or make room. Members joining after payment are covered from the next payment.

## 7. Refunds

### 7.1 We Refund

1. **Payment errors:** a duplicate payment, a wrong amount, or a payment we could not match — refunded or corrected in full.
2. **Declined requests:** if we decline a payment request, the amount is returned.
3. **First purchase:** within **7 days** of your first Plus approval, on request, once per Workspace.
4. **Our fault:** if an outage on our side makes the Workspace unusable for more than 72 hours in a row, or we discontinue Plus, we refund the unused months.
5. **Your legal rights:** nothing here limits refund rights you have under consumer law.

### 7.2 We Do Not Refund

Unused time after the first 7 days, except under Section 7.1; or a Workspace suspended for breaking the [Acceptable Use Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md) or [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

### 7.3 How to Ask

Email `info@ababilx.com` with the Workspace name and bKash transaction ID. We reply within 7 business days. Approved refunds are sent back to the bKash number that paid.

## 8. Price Changes

We may change the seat price for future payments. A change never affects months already paid, and we show the new price before you pay.

## 9. Records

Payment requests and their status are visible to the Workspace owner in billing. We keep payment records as long as tax law requires ([Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md), Section 12).
